Details

The dashed squares R1... (300x300 mm) are the opponent robots - this console reports them to the service like a camera does (count and positions in Config). Drag them anytime, also while your robot drives. The service reacts only when the other robot gets close to yours (~0.7 m): then it re-routes, or stops and waits until the way is clear, and continues by itself. A robot sitting on the path far ahead changes nothing - it is re-checked as your robot approaches. If a real camera streams on the vision channel, the console yields and that input is used. Box/Zone are static obstacles: they lock while the robot moves.

How the workflow works

Draw one step and Plan + Execute it - the robot always starts from where it stands, so every new plan covers only the new work. When the run finishes, validate it here: Add step appends it to the strategy (kept on the map, drawn dim green), Discard throws a test away. Save writes <name>.json - the brain plays exactly this file later, re-planning from the robot's real position. Load brings a saved strategy back to review or extend it. Run plans and drives the WHOLE strategy from the robot's current position (press it again to replay). Edit moves the strategy into the sequence so every tool modifies it - draw, erase, waypoints, order - then plan it with the normal buttons and Save writes it back to the file.

Details

Speed is the cruise speed of every planned move (the board ramps to it). Node step is the spacing of the waypoints on straight legs; Corner radius rounds every corner with a fillet of that radius (0 = sharp). Arc chord tol limits how far a curve may deviate from its true arc; Fit tolerance is how closely the fitted lines/arcs must follow your stroke. The switches: Fit lines & arcs (turn the stroke fitting off to keep the raw points), Avoid obstacles (route around Box/Zone and the opponents with Theta*), Round corners (apply the fillet smoothing).

Bench tests - idle

One-click bench and match-prep tests (STM32 bring-up checklist). Motion tests need floor clearance and a hand near E-STOP; each test lists its steps and shows PASS or FAIL with the numbers and the limits. All of them also run in MOCK (simulated) for rehearsals.

Pre-match readiness

Steps: 1 place the robot at its match start and press Set Pose, 2 press Check. Verifies: REAL link and firmware, latched faults, the recent board log, saved settings, the IMU (bias settled, stream active) and the robot standing at its start rectangle. Warnings do not fail; any FAIL means: do not start a match yet.

Square test (GOTO)

Steps: 1 clear a square of floor around the robot (side + robot + margin), 2 keep a hand near E-STOP, 3 press Run. Four absolute GOTOs drive the bench square; PASS = the robot returns within 20 mm / 2 deg of its start (odometry-based - confirm with a tape when calibrating).

Arc test

Steps: 1 clear a half-circle lane of floor (radius + robot + margin), 2 press Run. A left quarter arc then a right one; PASS = both end within 20 mm / 2 deg of the exact circle geometry (each arc is judged from its own start).

Chain audit (QUEUE_ARC)

Steps: 1 clear about 1.5 m of floor ahead of the robot, 2 press Run. The robot drives flat-arc -> quarter-arc -> flat-arc as ONE blended chain; PASS = zero mid-run stops (one acceleration, one deceleration). The closing error is reported for information; the usual "run finished: N segment(s), M blended" line lands in the log.

Rest-drift audit

Steps: 1 robot standing still with its wheels on the floor, nobody touching the table, 2 press Run and wait. Reports board and fused pose drift, encoder tick noise and gyro noise; PASS = board drift within 2 mm / 0.5 deg and no motion seen.

Turn repeatability audit

Steps: 1 robot standing with wheels on the floor, hands off, 2 press Run. Turns N times CCW then N times CW by the given angle; PASS = |mean| <= 3 deg and sigma <= 1.5 deg per direction for the fused heading. The scrub column shows how far the wheel-driven board heading diverges from the gyro-aided fused heading during turns.

Loop-drift audit

Steps: 1 clear floor for a square of the given side, 2 press Run. Drives the closed square loop repeatedly WITHOUT corrections and compares each source against the expected end pose: board drift = control + slip error, fused drift = what the estimator believes. PASS = last-loop fused drift within 60 mm / 4 deg.

Speed & ramp audit

Steps: 1 clear a straight lane of the given length, 2 press Run. Drives one straight at cruise while sampling the board's measured wheel speeds: reports the real peak and cruise speed, the time to 95 % of the command and the driven distance. PASS = peak within +/-15 % of the command and distance within 60 mm.

Encoder check

Steps: 1 clear the driving distance plus margin forward AND backward, 2 press Run. Drives +d, -d then turns +90 deg, reading the raw tick counters after every move: checks the tick scale against the board's odom calibration, the backward direction and the turn polarity (left negative / right positive, matching magnitudes).

Fault drills (safety rehearsal)

E-stop drill (no motion): latches the board e-stop, verifies the latch, clears it and verifies recovery. Deadman drill MOVES the robot: a slow creep of the given distance while telemetry AND the gyro stream go silent (any frame would feed the watchdog) - the board must stop ITSELF at the 1.5 s watchdog and latch DEADMAN. Run the deadman drill only with clear floor and a hand near E-STOP; the drill clears the fault before returning.

Board diagnostics - firmware status

Every failure the STM32 records (faults, rejected frames, flash and configuration events, boot cause) with its code and context byte.
